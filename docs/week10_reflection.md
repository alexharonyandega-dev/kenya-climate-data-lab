## 1. What was harder — building or writing?

Writing the 2-page insight paper was harder than building the dashboard. 

The dashboard took hours of fighting with Tableau's geocoder and UI, but it was a technical problem. There was a clear right answer: 47 dots on a map. 

Writing the 2-page summary required ruthless editing. I had to take a 3,000-word preprint and compress it into two pages without losing the honesty of the null result or the impact of the 31-vs-8 finding. I had to write for a non-technical audience (policymakers, teachers) while keeping the scientific integrity intact. 

## 2. What would I change in the paper?

If I were to revise it, I would add a clearer call to action at the bottom. Right now, the "Recommendations" section explains what policymakers *should* do, but I didn't include a direct link to the live dashboard inside the PDF itself. 

The PDF is static, but the dashboard is interactive. I should have put a QR code or a direct URL on the last page so the reader can immediately click through and explore the data themselves. I'll add that in the Week 11 revision.

## 3. Did the dashboard link get everywhere?

Yes. It is in the README, the Substack about page, the LinkedIn profile, and my email signature. The dashboard is now my public calling card. If someone asks "what does the project do?", I don't have to explain it anymore — I just send the link.
