# Advisory note — weekly maize stress monitor

**Received:** 2026-09-27  
**From:** Mark (external advisor)  
**Subject:** Review of Kenya Climate Data Lab approach

## Summary
Pivot from annual yield prediction toward a **weekly stress monitor**
for all 47 Kenyan counties.

## Recommended approach
1. Start with a weekly stress monitor, not yield prediction
2. Use free data: CHIRPS rainfall, Sentinel-2/MODIS NDVI, HDX boundaries
3. Know the crop calendar (west = 1 season, east = 2 seasons)
4. Only then train yield models (expect 20-30% error)
5. Show uncertainty ("below average, medium confidence")

## 2026 El Niño test case
- Western breadbasket: drought Apr-Sep, ~15% yield loss
- East and coast: Oct-Dec rains above average, flood risk
