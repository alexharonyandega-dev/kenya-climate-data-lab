\f0\fs24 \cf0 # Why Maize Yield Prediction?\
\
1. **Food security:** Maize is Kenya's staple crop, making it vital for the daily survival and nutrition of millions of households. Accurate predictions help foresee deficits early, preventing widespread food shortages and price shocks.\
2. **Data richness:** Multiple datasets are available (KMD, CHIRPS, FAOSTAT), providing high-quality historical rainfall, temperature, and yield data that make training robust machine learning models highly feasible.\
3. **Actionable:** County-level predictions can help farmers and policymakers make informed decisions, allowing local leaders to distribute resources efficiently and enabling farmers to adjust planting dates and inputs.\
4. **Vision 2030:** Aligns with Kenya's agricultural transformation goals by modernising farming through data-driven technologies, boosting smallholder productivity, and fostering national self-reliance.\
5. **Personal motivation:** I want to use data to solve climate challenges, bridging the gap between advanced data science and practical, real-world solutions that protect vulnerable communities from extreme weather patterns.\
